# PLAN 7.65: the reserve cut (ADR-615; ADR-592 a, the user's)

The rule: at war, while its army (with its orders in training) stands under its peacetime share,
a nation keeps no reserve of income back from its army's orders (`WAR_RESERVE_MONTHS` 0, was
RESERVE_MONTHS 3), and its armour's pick (`pickTemplate`) reads the same reserve. The 7.49e diff
(`docs/evidence/7.49/e/reserve-cut.diff`) changed the order's two gold tests only; with the pick
left at three months, a nation with the gold for a cheaper tank but not for the best one with
three months more fell to infantry. The warship's saving, the refills, the fleets and the wings keep
RESERVE_MONTHS.

## How

`sh docs/evidence/7.65/run.sh <label> <days>` (`tools/diag/purse.ts` on the critic's 48 saves of
run 11, year 10 and year 20, 4 shards side by side; `PURSE_WAR_RESERVE=0` for the rule's runs, so
the tool's reading of the gates matches), `node docs/evidence/7.65/sum.mjs <label>`. The band:
living nations at war whose month's gross income is 150–500 on the save. Labels: `head` (HEAD
`56f83362`, 180 days), `r0` (the rule, 180 days), `r0y` (the rule, 365 days).
