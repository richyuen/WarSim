# PLAN 7.47: a navy rebuilt to a ship or two saves for the next at war with a naval enemy (ADR-579)

The rule (`src/sim/ai/economic.ts`, `shipPrice`): ADR-557's saving (the army's orders and the
refills keep the cheapest warship's price of the nation's income's kinds over the reserve) held only
for a nation with no armed fleet; Britain's first destroyer rebuilt ended it. It now holds also for
a nation with armed fleets whose upkeep is under NAVY_SHARE of its income, while one of its wars has
an enemy with an armed fleet. In peace and in a war with no naval enemy, a nation with an armed
fleet does not save, as before.
