# PLAN 7.46b3: Japan's gold (traced; no rule, ADR-578 proposed)

The AT: Japan's `gold` months on 4 seeds × 10 years with the chase halved, from the chase alone's
76 / 48 / 65 / 88 (seeds 1307, 3613, 8693, 7019; 7.46a, `a/after_*`) to 38 / 24 / 32 / 44 or under.

## The run

`npx tsx tools/diag/navyRebuild.ts <seed> 10` in the chase worktree (`8c64c3c` +
`docs/evidence/7.39/b-chase.diff` + ADR-574 + ADR-575, the economic AI as at HEAD), JSON in `b3/`
(`run_*.json`), tables `node docs/evidence/7.46/b3/sum.mjs` (`b3/sum.txt`). The tool now traces each
`gold` month (the economic AI wrapped, as `navyLoss.ts` does; the sim unchanged): its kind
(`<lost|armed>/<peace|navalFoe|landFoe>/<overPeace|underPeace>`, the last the army with its orders in
training against its peacetime share, which gates ADR-557's saving at war), the treasury against
the line (the cheapest warship of its income's kinds and RESERVE_MONTHS of income), and what the
month's AI spent (land orders, refills, other orders). The figures are b2's (`b2/after_*`) to the
month: the tool reads, it does not change the run.

The task's text names `navyLoss.ts`; it traces lost navies only (no armed fleet), and 77 % of
Japan's gold months are of a navy with an armed fleet left, so the trace is `navyRebuild`'s.

Every one of the 262 is `underPeace`: Japan's army with its orders in training stands at 0.18 to
0.39 of its peacetime share on the mean of the seeds' gold months (at most 0.54). On 3613 its
upkeep is 57–165 against a peacetime line of 239–371 for ten years.

What spends the treasury (per gold month): the treasury stands at 0.87–0.91 of the line; the month's
refills take 73–284 a month on the mean; a land order in 3–10 of the months, a wing in 0–7. At war
the refills keep no price (ADR-514) unless the nation saves for a warship, so each month they
spend the surplus (a balance of 400–750) down to the reserve: the army's next division (1,001 over
the reserve) is not reached either. Japan's army does not recover from its war in China: it
neither raises divisions nor buys a warship while it bleeds.

- **armed / navalFoe (76):** a navy rebuilt to a ship or two at war with a naval enemy: ADR-557's
  saving ended at the first warship afloat. PLAN 7.47's case (Britain's 23 and 22 months, `b2.md`),
  for Japan too.
- **armed / landFoe (125) and lost / landFoe (19):** at war with no enemy that has an armed fleet,
  the army under its peacetime share. ADR-557 keeps that army first by design ("a war's army under
  it is not starved for a ship"); ADR-575 lifted it only for a war with a naval enemy.
- **lost / navalFoe (40):** the saving holds; these are the months of the climb to the line (a
  balance of 360–760 a month against a shortfall of 30–1,300), the line rising with the income
  (three months of it), and a wing ordered (step 3c keeps no warship's price; 6 of the 40 months
  had one, 252–378 gold).

## The bar against what a rule may do

A rule within ADR-557 (7.47's, and the climb shortened) takes at best every navalFoe month away,
and those would partly turn into warship-in-training months:

| seed | now | − navalFoe | bar | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1307 | 52 | 11 | 38 | met |
| 3613 | 82 | 63 | 24 | missed |
| 8693 | 62 | 38 | 32 | missed |
| 7019 | 66 | 34 | 44 | met |

The bar needs the land-war months: a depleted navy saving in a war with no naval enemy while its
army stands under its peacetime share, which amends ADR-557's floor at that army's cost. That is the
user's (ADR-578, proposed). No rule this task: the sim and the pin unchanged.
