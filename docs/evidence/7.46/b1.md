# PLAN 7.46b1: a navy's share counts its armed fleets (ADR-574)

The rule (`src/sim/ai/economic.ts`): the economic AI's two build tests of the navy's share, the
warship's order (3b) and ADR-557's saving, read the upkeep of the nation's armed fleets
(`fleetPower` > 0), not of every fleet. Submarine flotillas and transport groups (no armed power)
no longer stop a warship. The lay-up over the share, when short of money, reads every fleet as
before. `tools/diag/navyRebuild.ts` and `navyLoss.ts` read the share as the sim now does.
