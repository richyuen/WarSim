# PLAN 7.66: transports bought for a nation fronted through an ally alone (ADR-618)

2026-10-21, on HEAD `fce40df2` (before) and the rule (after). Tools: `tools/diag/islandArmies.ts`
(`noOwnFront`, 180 days of each of the 48 saves `critic/c11_seed*_y10.bin`, `_y20.bin`, `MID=60`,
in four runs of 12: `7.66/before_y*.json`, `after_y*.json`, their `.log`s),
`npm run sim -- --scenario 1938 --seed 99 --years 2 --profile --affinity 0xFFFF`
(`7.66/profile_99.log`).

## 1. The rule

`wantsFerry` (`ai/economic.ts`, ADR-614): a nation with no front cell of its own takes as its front
its allies' cells beside the enemies it fights with them (`wars.together`), as the ferry does since
ADR-617. The rest of the want as before: a port of its or an ally's on that front's land, an island
army (on a land with a port of its own and no front) of more men than its transports' room.

Test: `ferry1938.test.ts`, a new case. The United Kingdom and its allies at war with Italy; every
British cell beside an enemy handed to an ally; one transport group of a division's room (the
landing's want, `wantsTransport`, then false: asserted); two divisions at Glasgow, more men than the
room. The want holds, and the month's start queues a transport group. Without the rule both the
want and the order are red (each checked alone: with no group at all the landing's want orders one
whatever the front, so the case keeps one).

## 3. The named cases: armies on a land with no port of their own

The task named the Netherlands and the British Raj on 8693 year 20 (no transports at all). With the
rule, on that save, the want is still false for both, read condition by condition:

- the Raj: no front cell of its own; its allies' front on land 56 (Eurasia), where it has its
  ports and allies' ports to land at; its 5 island formations stand on land 531, where it holds
  **no port**. No "home" port to board at, so no want and no ferry.
- the Netherlands: its allies' front on land 56 too, but no port of its or an ally's there with
  water for a landing; its 5 formations on land 529, also with no port of its own (its ports are
  on nine other lands).

Both lands are islets of their own (529 one cell, 531 four), with no port of any nation (read on
the same save after the commit). So the named cases are armies standing on a landmass where their nation holds no port: no transport
can take them aboard (the ferry boards at a port of the nation's own on the army's land, ADR-612). A different cause from
7.66's, filed as PLAN 7.67.
