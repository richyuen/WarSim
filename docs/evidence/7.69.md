# PLAN 7.69: the AI's landing on an enemy capital on a coast with no port (ADR-621)

2026-10-21. Before: HEAD `63416db6` (7.68's rule). After: the rule. Tool: `tools/diag/capitalLandings.ts`
(new): 180 days of each of the critic's 48 saves (`critic/c11_seed*_y10.bin`, `_y20.bin`), four runs
of 12 (`7.69/before_y*.json`, `7.69/after_y*.json`, their `.log`s).

The cases, read at each save's start: per war, a living nation whose capital (`capitalCell`, a field
capital too) stands on a coast (a cell beside it of a sea zone) of a landmass where it controls no
port, while a nation of the other side has transport room afloat and no land front with the
capital's side in that war. One case per (save, capital). A capital inland is no case: no rule of a
landing on a coast reaches it (the read's first cut counted Afghanistan's and Mongolia's). Landed:
a `FormationLanded` of a nation of the other side on that landmass within 180 days.

## 1. The rule

`planLanding` (`ai/invasion.ts`): beside the enemies' ports, each enemy's capital held by one of those
enemies, at the sea distance (`seaKm`) of a water cell beside it whose zone passes a port's tests
(open, not iced, in the transports' waters, not held by a nation at war with the lander); nearest
first, a port winning a tie. `orderLanding` goes ashore by the target as before (the nearest zoned
water, the target's land nearest it).

Test: `landingOnCapital1938.test.ts`. The United Kingdom at war with the Netherlands alone, every
cell of the Netherlands' side (its puppet's too) but a portless islet handed to Britain, the Dutch
capital a field capital on the islet, British transports at Manama with a division: on Britain's
invasion day the division goes aboard bound for the islet. Red without the rule (nobody boards: no
target).
