# PLAN 7.68: a capital moves to the land its nation holds most of (ADR-620)

2026-10-21. Before: HEAD `3f672059` (7.67's rule). After: the rule. Tool: `tools/diag/capitalMoves.ts`
(new): 180 days of each of the critic's 48 saves (`critic/c11_seed*_y10.bin`, `_y20.bin`), four runs
of 12 (`7.68/before_y*.json`, `7.68/after_y*.json`, their `.log`s). Each `CapitalMoved` to a city is
read after its hour: the city's landmass (`navOf(world).grid.component`) against the landmass where
its nation owns and controls the most cells (`onMost`), and whether its nation owned and controlled
a city on that landmass then (`possible`).

## 1. The rule

`relocateCapital` (`systems/capitals.ts`): of the cities the nation owns and controls, those on the
landmass where it owns and controls the most cells (of the landmasses with such a city; lowest
landmass id on ties; a cell of no landmass, 0, counts for none); of those the largest, lowest id on
ties, as before. Without a city, a field capital as before. Its five callers (capture, a capital
given away, two in revolts, the editor) take it alike.

Test: `capitals.test.ts`, a new case. Japan at war with the Soviet Union, Tokyo and every other
Japanese city of its size on Honshu taken: the largest city Japan holds stands in Korea (land 56,
858 cells), and the capital moves to Honshu's largest left (land 378, 1,389 cells). Red without the
rule: 125 (Korea), not 207.

At the 1938 start, a capital lost at once goes to the same city under both rules for every living
nation but the Philippines (old: city 271, size 4, a land of 251 cells; new: 758, size 3, 300
cells; `tools/zz`, not kept). Denmark (Greenland) and the Netherlands (Suriname) go where they went
before: their largest city is on the land they hold most of.
