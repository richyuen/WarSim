## 1. The rule

`invasionAi` (`ai/invasion.ts`): in a nation's turn (its actor day, none of its formations aboard),
when no landing has a target (no war with no front, or no enemy port to sail for), a nation with a
land front ships its free formations standing on a landmass with none of its front cells (`planFerry`):

- its transports as a landing's (the group with the most room at a port of its own);
- standing on a front's land, or on a land with none of that army, they sail for a port of its on
  the army's land that one of the four formations nearest them can march to (`sailToArmy`);
- else to its port on a front's land nearest the transports by sea (open, unbombed, in the waters
  they sail, not held by its enemy): its formations at their port go aboard, or the nearest on the
  port's land are sent there (`board`, the landing's own steps, now shared).

Test: `ferry1938.test.ts`, the United Kingdom at war with Italy (a front in Africa), a division at
Glasgow: it goes aboard for a British port on a front's land; with the transports at a British port
there, they sail for a British port on Britain. Both red without the rule.
