## 1. The rule

`planFerry` (`ai/invasion.ts`): when no port of the nation's own on a land of its front cells passes
the ferry's tests (water, open zone, unbombed, in the transports' waters, its sea not an enemy's),
it takes an ally's: of the ports held by a nation fighting beside it (`wars.sameSide`) that pass
the same tests, the nearest a front cell of its own on that land (straight cells), the nearest by
sea on a tie. The landing there is a landing at its own port: `land` takes a cell from an enemy
alone, and a nation fighting beside it is not foreign ground (`foreignTo`), so nobody marches home
from it.

Test: `ferry1938.test.ts`, third case: the United Kingdom at war with Italy, its allies in; its
ports on its fronts' lands handed to an ally in the war; a division at Glasgow goes aboard for the
ally's port on a front's land. Red without the rule (nobody aboard).

## 3. Why it misses (`ferryAlly.ts`, `d2/why_y*.json`, day 1 of each save)

Each island formation of a nation with no own port on its front's land, under the first of:

| | year 10 | year 20 | the 48 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no front cell of its own (its land front an ally's alone) | 281 | 512 | **793** |
| no transport group with room | 6 | 41 | 47 |
| transports, none at a port of its own | 0 | 22 | 22 |
| no ally's port on its front cells' lands | 28 | 159 | 187 |
| an ally's port there (the rule's case) | 10 | 0 | **10** |

`islandArmies.ts` counts a nation's front with its allies' cells (`landStates.ts`'s way: a nation
fighting beside a front counts on it), and its `noOwnPort` with no front cell of its own as no
port. The ferry, as the landing, takes a nation's own front cells (`invasionAi`'s `frontCells`): a
nation whose land front is an ally's alone has none, and `planFerry` is never called for it. Of
the figure, 74 % is that case (Latvia's 40 on 5309 year 20; most are small nations beside a
great power's front). The rule's own case is 10 formations, 1 %.

The two counts: 7.49d §3's 217 (year 20) tested the ports against the lands of the front with the
allies' cells in; `noOwnPort` tests them against the lands of the nation's own front cells, so a
nation with none of its own is "no own port" by construction (734 at year 20). The 793 are that
difference: what holds them is that `planFerry` is never called, not their ports.

The 187 with no ally's port, the Soviet Union's 158 on 5309 year 20 among them (7.49d's named case):
front cells of its own, transports with room at a port of its own, and no port of its own or of an
ally's (with water) on the land of its front cells. The ferry needs a port there to sail for; d2
cannot reach them and 7.64 does not cover them. Where that front stands, and what a ferry could
land at instead (a coast with no port, an enemy's port in the same war), is not traced.
